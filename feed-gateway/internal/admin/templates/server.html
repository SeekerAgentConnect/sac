Add a credential (rotation)

The new credential works immediately and the existing ones keep working, so the publisher can switch without downtime. Revoke the old one once it has.

Revoke every credential

This publisher stops being able to publish from the next request. Its manifest and the feed items it already published stay on the feed — revocation ends publishing, it does not withdraw what was published.

Forget publisher

This removes the registration, its credentials, its manifest and every feed item this gateway holds for it. It is not a rotation and it is not a revocation: the public feed for this channel stops existing.

Phones that already read its feed items keep their own copies until their owners remove the feed. This clears what the gateway holds, not what a device decided.
