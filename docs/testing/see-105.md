# SEE-105 notification presentation verification

SEE-105 changes presentation only. The authoritative Sync/read happens first, FCM payloads remain
content-free invalidations, and notification taps remain immutable ID-only routes that fetch
current state. No notification action can answer, approve, sign, send, prepare, or open a wallet.

## What changed

- `ic_notification_sac` is a transparent, monochrome vector derived from the approved SAC mark for
  Android's status-bar and shade mask. Both builders and Firebase's manifest default use it rather
  than a launcher/adaptive icon or a framework placeholder.
- The existing full-colour lime launcher resource is the native large icon. The approved deep
  olive light-surface accent and lime dark-surface accent are resource-qualified so Android keeps
  control of notification backgrounds and typography.
- Request titles distinguish acknowledgement, signature, transfer, swap and unknown review work.
  Feed titles distinguish swap, prediction and a future unknown signal without exposing raw enum
  or protocol names. The source is always the owner's local connection name, with an honest
  `Unnamed connection` fallback for unreadable legacy data.
- The collapsed view has a title, source and concise summary. Native `BigTextStyle` retains the
  complete source and summary for long names and larger system text. No custom `RemoteViews` is
  used.
- Existing per-item tags, notification IDs, `setOnlyAlertOnce`, channels, permission behavior,
  secret lock-screen visibility, cancellation, and immutable tap destinations are unchanged.

## Automated evidence

`RequestNotificationsTest` and `FeedNotificationsTest` cover the SAC small and large resources,
the light/dark accent resources, native expanded text, every known type plus an unknown type, long
and missing source names, authoritative-record gating, multiple items from two sources, unchanged
deduplication/cancellation, secret visibility, and exact immutable routes. They also prove private
request text and proposal notes/identifiers are absent from display copy. `StageBoundaryTest` pins
the Firebase defaults and keeps notification code free of wallet, approval, worker, or background
authority.

Android SDK. Go was unpacked to an isolated temporary directory because Homebrew's prefix was not

## Physical Seeker checklist

Record the exact commit, Seeker model, Android build, APK variant, Firebase configuration, system
theme, font/display size, and permission/channel state. Attach before/after physical-device
screenshots to SEE-105; do not substitute a Robolectric result, APK build, emulator, or design
mockup.

1. Post one acknowledgement, signature and transfer request. For each, capture collapsed and
   expanded views and confirm the SAC silhouette is clear, the app identity is recognizable, and
   the title/source/summary hierarchy matches the current request.
2. Repeat in light and dark system themes, then at the largest system text size and with a 64-code
   point connection name. Nothing essential may disappear from the expanded view or overlap.
3. Post multiple requests from two differently named connections. Each alert must identify the
   right source, retain its own tap destination, alert only once, and cancel independently.
4. Repeat for swap and prediction feed signals. Raw operation/plugin IDs, publisher notes and
   proposal terms must not appear in the notification.
5. With the app backgrounded and after ordinary process death, deliver real FCM invalidations.
   Refined appearance must survive; a tap must fetch and open the exact current item without a
   wallet prompt or answer. Repeat with expired, canceled and already answered items.
6. Inspect the secure lock screen and channel settings. Content remains secret and the existing
   permission, channels, badges and priorities remain functional.
