## Not changed

- Manual approval, no automatic signing, no Warn/Block mode, no hard cap.
- Policy evaluation of every non-daily check, consent binding, input validation precedence.
- Fees are not added to thresholds and never invented.

## Known limitations

- Operations recorded before this build carry no spending; they are not reconstructed from chosen
  parameters or the proposal, so on their own local day they leave the daily total for their
  wallet and chain unknown (`daily_total_unverified`). Staking records from before SEE-165 (no
  wallet or action) are unknown for every scope on their day.
- Per-request thresholds still apply to the moved amount of a staking withdrawal; only the daily
  accounting treats it as incoming.
- Counters are app-local: nothing done in the wallet directly or by another app is seen.
