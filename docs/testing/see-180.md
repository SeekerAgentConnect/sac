# SEE-180 verification record

Date: 2026-10-02

SEE-180 stops the prediction review from warning about a transaction that does not exist, makes a
refused preparation one clear card with **Try again**, and compacts the sheet into the decision plus
four collapsed sections. This records what was verified, how, and what was not.

## Root cause, as found

With nothing prepared, `actionFacts` builds `RequestFacts.unread` (`fullyRead = false`), and
`withVerificationPrecedence` correctly withholds ALLOWED as `RequestUnverified`. The bug was in the
reading: `PolicyDecision.reviewVerdict()` showed that reason as a warning, attributed it to
`RuleSource.Global` when no rule document was unreadable, and the prediction mapper asked for the
"approve anyway" tick for any orange card, prepared or not. The evaluation is unchanged; only the
reading and the sheet are.

## Not changed

- Policy evaluation (`withVerificationPrecedence`), consent binding, `OperationViewModel.approve`
  and the execution binding gate.
- Other review screens: `ReviewSheet` is used in the app only by the prediction review, every new
  `ReviewSheetState` field defaults to the old rendering, and the designsystem/app Roborazzi
  goldens verify unchanged. The swap review (`ProposalReviewScreen`) does not use
  `reviewVerdict`. `ReviewVerdict` callers keep `prepared = true` by default; only the RequestUnverified row's
  attribution changed (now **Transaction check**).

## Design guide

The design export has no disclosure row, preparation-error notice or verification scope chip. They
are built from existing tokens and the rules sheet's expandable-intro pattern; a design refresh
(`design/UPDATING.md`) should add references for them as a separate design-only change.
