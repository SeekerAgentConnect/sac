# SEE-175 verification record

Date: 2026-09-29

SEE-175 keeps a feed's backlog, the catch-up after returning to the app, and bursts of requests from
turning into a queue of foreground banners. It changes nothing about what is stored, listed,
counted or unread, nothing about system notifications, and nothing about approval or rules.

## Emulator (not a Seeker device)

Run on the `sac` AVD (Android 35) against the deployed seeker-mcp, following
`docs/development/emulator-e2e.md`, with this branch's debug build:

1. **Catch-up.** Paired, sent the app to the background, created 6 requests, brought it back: the
   count went to 6 and **no banner** appeared over the next 16 s (the old code queued one banner per
   request here).
2. **Burst.** 10 requests fired in parallel in the foreground: **one** banner, "10 new requests,
   open inbox"; count 6 → 16.
3. **Sustained, with dismissal.** One request every 2.5 s for 75 s. The first, isolated, got its own
   detailed banner; it was swiped away at 2 s and nothing reappeared until 32 s, when one banner
   said "13 new requests" (merging to 14 while visible, then leaving on time); the next came at
   67 s. The count rose by one every 2.5 s throughout (16 → 46).
4. **Navigation.** Tapping a "3 new requests" banner opened Inbox → Pending.

All 49 test requests were cancelled afterwards. The feed backlog could not be exercised on the
emulator: the CopyTrading demo had no open signals and its admin login was refused, so the feed
path rests on the unit tests above.
