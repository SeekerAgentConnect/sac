# SEE-147 verification record

Date: 2026-09-23

SEE-147 adds the foreground in-app notification banner for a new request, a new signal, and a
paired server that ends the pairing. It changes nothing about who can approve, sign, or send, and
nothing about background delivery.

## Coverage

| Foreground versus background | Collection runs inside `repeatOnLifecycle(STARTED)`. `InAppNotificationHostTest.what arrived while the app was away is not replayed as banners when it returns` drives a real `LifecycleRegistry` down to `CREATED` and back: the banner goes with the foreground, two arrivals while away announce nothing on return, and the next arrival after that does. `InAppNotificationSourceTest` covers the same rule directly. No existing system-notification path changed. |
| TalkBack | `InAppNotificationHostTest.TalkBack announces the arrival politely and offers Dismiss instead of the swipe` asserts `LiveRegionMode.Polite`, finds the custom action by its label, invokes it, and asserts the banner goes without opening anything. Labels are asserted per kind in the three routing tests. |

Nineteen new tests. No protocol schema, generated code, security authority, or RFC scope changes.

## One bug found and fixed on the way

The banner's drag read its pointer from inside the layer it was translating, so each move's reported
travel was cancelled by the previous move's translation and the drag tracked the finger at half
speed: a 72dp swipe needed 144dp of finger. The threshold tests found it at three different step
counts, and the gesture now sits outside the moving layer. It was never reachable outside SEE-147's
own banner.
