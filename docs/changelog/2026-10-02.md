# 2026-10-02

## Fixed

- **A prediction review no longer warns about a transaction that does not exist (SEE-180).** Before
  a side and amount were chosen, and after a preparation failed, the sheet showed "Needs attention ·
  1 warning — This phone could not account for the whole transaction" under a **Global rule** chip,
  with an "approve anyway" tick. Nothing had been prepared: unread facts made the (correctly
  conservative) policy decision `RequestUnverified`, and the verdict reading attributed that to the
  global rules. `reviewVerdict(prepared)` now reads the absence of a transaction as `Pending` — no
  card, no tick, no tile warning — while a failed check, unreadable rules or an unknown day's total
  are still shown under their real source. Once bytes exist, a transaction the phone cannot account
  for is a blocker attributed to the new **Transaction check** origin, never to a rule, and no tick
  gets past it. Policy evaluation, consent binding and every approval gate are unchanged.
- **A refused preparation is one clear card with Try again.** "Insufficient funds — Could not
  prepare the 5 USDC order. The provider reported insufficient funds." keeps the chosen side and
  amount and offers **Try again** (prepares again for the bound wallet; never signs). Other refusals
  use the provider's explanation. The provider's code and words are under Technical details. The
  quote placeholder no longer says "Choose a side and an amount" once both are chosen. Core
  recognises the refusal through the new boundary code `PluginFailureCodes.INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`.
- **SEE-180 review follow-ups (PR #101).** A Home signal tile no longer shows a green **In rules**
  pill for a signal nobody has prepared: `ReviewVerdict.Pending` (and a signal not yet assessed)
  now sets `RequestTileModel.rulesPending`, which leaves the pill out, and
  `reviewVerdict(prepared = false)` never reads an allowed decision as within the rules. While
  **Refresh quote** or **Get a new quote** is with the provider, the sheet shows the loading card
  instead of the expired or refused quote's rows, verdict or blocker. The provider's explanation and
  its own words under Technical details are wrapping blocks rather than one-line rows that clipped
  a long message.
- **Daily limits count feed swaps, prediction buys and SKR stakes (SEE-181).** The day's counters
  read only direct transfers, so a 20 USDC prediction buy repeated six times under a 100 USDC daily
  threshold never warned. Every execution now pins what its inspected bytes spend
  (`ReviewedSpending`: a swap's input in its own asset, a prediction's deposit) in the binding before
  the wallet is opened, and the counters read it back from Activity: unresolved until the chain
  settles it, then confirmed, under the same identity so it is never counted twice. A stake counts;
  an unstake, a cancellation and a withdrawal do not, and a withdrawal neither counts nor replenishes
  the day. Outputs, payouts and refunds are never spending. Global and connection scopes, local
  days, integer base units and deduplication are unchanged. Operations recorded before this build
  do not say what they spent: on their own day they make the daily total for their wallet and chain
  unknown — "1 of today's operations were recorded without what they spent" — rather than zero.
- **PR #102 review follow-ups (SEE-181).** A gasless prediction buy — Jupiter's sponsor pays the
  network fee — was counted against the sponsor's address, because the inspection reported the fee
  payer as the order's wallet; it now names the verified owner the deposit leaves, so the buy adds
  up with that owner's transfers and swaps in both evaluation and the persisted record (covered with
  the real captured gasless order). A sandbox rehearsal the app closed on between binding and being
  recorded as simulated was turned Unknown on restart and counted as unresolved exposure; the
  counters now exclude any operation bound in the sandbox environment, whatever its outcome.
- **A feed approval that waited for the wallet is judged against what was spent meanwhile
  (SEE-181).** A swap or prediction approval now reloads the rules and Activity after waiting for the
  wallet lock, as a transfer's already did; a verdict or total that moved stops it before anything is
  bound, shows the new assessment and asks again (with a fresh tick if it now warns).

## Changed

- **No rules is neutral everywhere (SEE-181).** A connection with no rules is shown as "No rules
  configured · not checked" — on the transfer and swap reviews (neutral card instead of the orange
  "outside your rules"), the prediction sheet (a neutral line instead of an orange verdict and a
  tick), Home tiles (no pill instead of "Outside rules", and no "In rules" for a request nothing
  checked) and History. It never asks for the "approve anyway" tick. A match now reads "Configured
  checks passed" and names the checks no rule covers; an exceeded limit is one line with the amount
  against the threshold ("Over the global daily limit: 120 USDC of 100 USDC today."). Rules editor
  copy says a limit warns and never caps spending or signs. Design system: `RequestTileModel` takes
  `unchecked` in place of `outsideRules`/`rulesPending`, and `VerdictPillVerdict.OutsideRules` is
  gone (it was never in the design guide). See [`docs/policy.md`](../policy.md#what-counts-as-spending).
- **The prediction review is compact (SEE-180).** The decision stays open — market, where the order
  goes and when it closes on one line, network, your side and amount, the verdict when it has
  something true to say, the quote or the card that replaces it, and the wallet — with the pinned
  footer unchanged. Everything else moved, nothing was dropped, into four collapsed sections:
  **Limits and checks** ("Daily limits not set" instead of two "Not configured" rows), **About this
  order and risks**, **Jupiter and terms** (attribution, fees, regions, non-endorsement, terms and
  privacy links) and **Technical details** (identifiers that copy, the market link, supporting
  figures). The "whole operation, quoted here" heading is now "Quote", the market ID left the
  sub-line, and the amount sheet shows the market's minimum beside the field. Design system:
  `ReviewSheetSection`, `ReviewSheetPreparationError`, a nullable verdict, `TermsCard(title)`,
  `NoticeCard(title)` + `NoticeCardKind.PreparationFailed`, `ScopeChipSource.Verification`; existing
  goldens are unchanged. Plugins may mark a `PluginFact` as `technical` and a `ProviderNote` with a
  `topic`. See [`docs/wiki/prediction-review.md`](../wiki/prediction-review.md) and the before/after
  screenshots in [`docs/testing/see-180.md`](../testing/see-180.md).
