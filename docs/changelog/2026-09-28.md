# 2026-09-28

## Added

- **Prediction positions are followed and sold in-app from the original History item (SEE-172).**
  A sent prediction purchase's History item now shows a *Position now* card: the order's fill as
  Jupiter reports it (pending, partly filled, filled, partly filled with the rest returned, failed —
  apart from the chain result), the wallet's whole position in that outcome read live (contracts,
  value, best bid, cost basis, P&L, market status, last read; stale reads kept and marked, a 404 shown
  as *not found yet*, never as zero), each sale attempt, Refresh, and the Jupiter links. **Sell
  position** opens a review of a whole-position close built by Jupiter and read from its bytes — side,
  position, owner, exact quantity, floor price, proceeds destination, fees, every instruction — and
  hands exactly those bytes to the wallet once. Sales are their own records, reconciled by reading
  (chain confirmation, order fill, remaining position), never re-sent. Positions shared by several
  purchases are read once and shown as one aggregate. See `docs/wiki/prediction-positions.md` and
  `docs/research/2026-09-28-prediction-positions.md`.
- `scripts/capture-jupiter.mjs --positions` captures a real position, order status, a 404, a close
  and a NO buy with their lookup tables into `fixtures/jupiter/positions.json`.

## Fixed

- **NO-side prediction buys were refused.** The order instruction's flags are `isYes, isBuy` on the
  wire; the reader had them swapped, which only a YES buy survives.
- **Gasless Jupiter builds were refused.** Jupiter's relayer now pays and pre-signs; the buy and sale
  reviews accept a pre-signed non-owner fee payer while still requiring the owner's to be the only
  missing signature.

## Changed

- Jupiter answers `status` queries (`statusQueries = true`) with the order's reported fill.
